Gurpreet Singh
Heavy Vehicle Driver, Ludhiana

I hold a heavy transport vehicle licence and have been driving trailers and multi-axle trucks for 9 years.
At VRL Logistics I drive long-haul routes between Ludhiana, Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru carrying goods up to 30 tonnes.
Before every trip I check the brakes, tyres, lights, oil and air pressure and note any problems in the log.
I tie down and cover the load with ropes and tarpaulin so nothing shifts on the highway.
I plan routes and stops, keep the e-way bill, LR and other papers ready at check posts, and maintain trip logs.
I have carried hazardous chemicals with the proper permits and safety kit.
No accidents in 9 years. I can do basic repairs like changing a tyre or fixing a loose belt on the road.
Education: 10th pass, Punjab School Education Board.
