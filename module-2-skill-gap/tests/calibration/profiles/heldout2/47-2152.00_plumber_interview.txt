Q: What work have you done?
A: I have been a plumber for 12 years in Hyderabad, first with a contractor and now on my own.
I lay water supply lines in new houses with CPVC and GI pipes and connect the overhead tank and the borewell motor.
I fit bathrooms and kitchens - taps, sinks, closets, showers, geysers.
When there is a leak or a blocked drain I find where it is and repair it, sometimes by cutting the wall.
I can read a simple plumbing drawing and estimate the material for a flat.
