Aisha Khan works as a guest service associate at the front desk of a 120-room business hotel in Hyderabad.
She checks guests in and out, assigns rooms, issues key cards and collects payment or verifies the company billing.
She takes reservations over the phone and from booking sites, answers questions about the hotel and the city, and arranges cabs and wake-up calls.
When guests complain she sorts it out with housekeeping or maintenance and follows up.
She uses the IDS Fortune property management system and holds a diploma in hotel management.
