Priyanka Sen is an Assistant Engineer in the Public Works Department, Government of West Bengal, posted to the bridges division in Kolkata.
She designs reinforced concrete culverts and small bridges, checks the structural calculations submitted by consultants, and prepares estimates as per the schedule of rates.
She inspects bridges before and after the monsoon, records cracks and settlement, and recommends repairs.
During construction she checks that the concrete grade, reinforcement and foundation levels follow the approved drawings and the IRC codes, and she certifies the measurements for contractor payment.
She is a B.Tech in Civil Engineering from Jadavpur University and uses STAAD Pro and AutoCAD.
