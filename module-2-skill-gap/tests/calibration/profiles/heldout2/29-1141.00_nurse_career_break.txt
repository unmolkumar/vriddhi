Shalini D'Souza - returning to nursing after a career break

I worked as a staff nurse in the surgical ward of St. John's Medical College Hospital, Bengaluru from 2012 to 2018, then took five years off to look after my children.
On the ward I handled pre- and post-operative patients, checked their pulse, BP and oxygen levels through the shift and reported anything worrying to the resident.
I gave injections and IV fluids, changed dressings on surgical wounds and kept the drain and catheter charts.
I also explained to patients and relatives how to care for the wound and which tablets to take after going home.
In 2024 I completed a three-month refresher course with clinical posting, renewed my Karnataka Nursing Council registration and my BLS.
Qualification: B.Sc Nursing, 2012.
