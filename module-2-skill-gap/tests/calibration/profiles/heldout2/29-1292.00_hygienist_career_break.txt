Back to work after a 4-year break (moved cities, small child).
Earlier I was a dental hygienist for six years at a dental chain in Pune. I did scaling and polishing, applied fluoride and pit-and-fissure sealants for children, recorded gum health on the chart and took dental X-rays for the dentist. I taught patients how to brush and floss properly and how diet affects their teeth. I kept the instruments sterile and the chair area clean.
I recently did a refresher workshop on infection control and ultrasonic scaling. Diploma in Dental Hygiene, 2013.
