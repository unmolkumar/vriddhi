Mohammed Irfan is a clinical pharmacist at the District Government Hospital, Malappuram, where he has worked since 2019.
He checks the medicine orders written for inpatients, looks out for wrong doses and drug combinations that shouldn't be given together, and calls the doctor when something needs changing.
He runs the hospital's adverse drug reaction reporting under the national pharmacovigilance programme.
At the dispensing counter he explains to patients how to take their tablets, inhalers and insulin pens.
He also manages the indent and storage of medicines from the state medical services corporation and keeps the narcotic stock register.
He holds a Pharm.D from JSS College of Pharmacy, Ooty.
