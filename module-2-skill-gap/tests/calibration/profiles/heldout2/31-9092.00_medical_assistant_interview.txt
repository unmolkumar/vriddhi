Q: Tell me about yourself.
A: I am Kavya, I have worked for three years as a medical assistant in a multi-speciality clinic in Vijayawada.
In the morning I set up the consulting rooms and check that the BP machine, thermometer and glucometer work.
When patients come, I take their history in short, check their vitals and blood sugar, and enter everything in the clinic software before the doctor sees them.
I fix appointments, call patients to remind them about follow-ups and prepare the bills.
I also collect samples for the lab, give injections when the doctor writes them, and do ECGs.
At the end of the day I clean the instruments and send them for sterilisation.
