Q. What is your current job?
A. Customer service executive in the health insurance helpline of a large insurer, for four years.
Q. What calls do you handle?
A. Policy queries, claim status, cashless hospital lists and premium payment problems.
Q. How do you deal with an angry caller?
A. I listen fully, check the policy and claim in the system and tell them exactly what happens next.
Q. Do you close the issue yourself?
A. Most of them; the rest I raise to the claims team and call the customer back with the update.
