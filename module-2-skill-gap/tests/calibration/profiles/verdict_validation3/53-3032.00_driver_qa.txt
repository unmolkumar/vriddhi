Q: How long have you been driving trucks?
A: Fifteen years, mostly 14-wheel trailers carrying steel coils from Jamshedpur.
Q: What do you check before a trip?
A: Tyre pressure, brakes, oil, lights and the chains holding the load.
Q: How do you plan long routes?
A: I choose the route with the owner, plan rest stops and keep the trip sheet and diesel bills.
Q: Any accidents?
A: None in the last ten years.
