Annamma has worked as a nurse in the dialysis unit of a private hospital in Kottayam for eight years. Before each session she checks the patient's weight, blood pressure and fistula, then connects them to the machine and sets the fluid removal as the nephrologist has ordered. During the four hours she watches for cramps and falling pressure and acts quickly when they happen. She gives the injections prescribed for anaemia, writes up every session in the chart and explains diet and fluid limits to patients and their families.
