Q: What kind of design do you do?
A: Brand identity and packaging for food and cosmetic brands, six years at a studio in Mumbai.
Q: How do you start a project?
A: I read the brief, collect references, sketch ideas and then build two or three directions in Illustrator.
Q: And after the client picks one?
A: I refine it, prepare the final artwork and check colour proofs with the printer.
