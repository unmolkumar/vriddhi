Mrs. D'Souza has taught English to classes 9 to 12 at an ICSE school in Goa for fourteen years. She plans each term's lessons around the syllabus, sets and corrects essays and grammar tests, and keeps careful records of every student's marks. Weak students get extra help after school, and she meets parents regularly to talk about progress. She also runs the school debate club and helps prepare students for the board exams.
