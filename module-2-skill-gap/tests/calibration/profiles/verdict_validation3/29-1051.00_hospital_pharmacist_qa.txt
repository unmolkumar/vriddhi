Q: Where do you work?
A: In-patient pharmacy of a 500-bed hospital in Hyderabad, five years as pharmacist.
Q: What is your main job?
A: I check each prescription for dose and interactions, dispense medicines to the wards and keep the narcotics register.
Q: Do you talk to doctors?
A: Yes, I call them when a dose looks wrong or a drug is out of stock and suggest an alternative.
Q: Anything else?
A: I counsel patients going home about how to take their medicines.
