Chef Imran leads the kitchen of a 120-cover North Indian restaurant in Lucknow. He decides the menu with the owner every season, works out what each dish costs to make and fixes the selling price. Each morning he checks the vegetables and meat that arrive and sends back anything that is not fresh. He divides the work between six cooks, shows new boys how to make the gravies the house way and tastes every curry before service. He also keeps the cold room temperature log and makes sure the kitchen passes the FSSAI inspection.
