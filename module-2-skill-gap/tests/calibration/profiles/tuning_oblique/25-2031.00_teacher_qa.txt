Question: Which classes do you teach?
Answer: Mathematics for class 9 and 10 at a state board school in Mysuru, for eleven years.
Question: How do you plan your lessons?
Answer: I break the syllabus into weekly plans and prepare worksheets and examples from daily life.
Question: How do you know if students are learning?
Answer: I give short tests every Friday, correct them the same week and take extra classes for those who score low.
Question: Anything else?
Answer: I meet parents every term to discuss progress and I am in charge of the school's maths olympiad team.
