Ritu has spent five years on the customer helpline of a broadband company in Noida. She takes about eighty calls a shift from people whose internet is down or whose bill looks wrong, and she walks them through router resets before raising a technician visit. When a customer is angry she stays calm, notes the complaint in the ticketing tool and promises a callback time she can keep. Her team lead often hands her the escalations because she explains plan changes and refunds in simple Hindi and English. She was the floor's top scorer on quality audits twice last year.
