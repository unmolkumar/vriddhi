Over seven years at a cooperative bank in Kolhapur, Sunil has become the person farmers and shopkeepers ask for when they need credit. He visits the applicant's shop or farm, looks at their sales books and land records, and decides how much they can repay each month. He prepares the loan note for the branch committee, explaining the risk and the security offered. Once a loan is sanctioned he completes the documents, and he keeps an eye on repayments, phoning borrowers as soon as an instalment is late.
