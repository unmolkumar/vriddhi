For four years Farah has handled the email and chat queue of an online travel agency in Gurugram. She answers questions about bookings, cancellations and refunds, checks the reservation in the system before replying, and processes changes the customer asks for. When a flight is cancelled she rebooks the traveller and explains the refund timeline. Cases she cannot close are passed to the airline desk with full notes, and she follows up until the customer confirms it is sorted.
