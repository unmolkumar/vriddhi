Q. Tell us about your current role.
A. I am the accounts executive at a garment exporter in Tiruppur and handle the books single-handed for six years.
Q. What does a month look like?
A. I post purchase and sales entries in Tally, match the bank statement, pay vendors and prepare the salary sheet.
Q. And taxes?
A. I file GST returns every month and work out the TDS on contractor payments and rent.
Q. What about the year end?
A. I prepare the trial balance and schedules and sit with the auditor until the accounts are signed.
