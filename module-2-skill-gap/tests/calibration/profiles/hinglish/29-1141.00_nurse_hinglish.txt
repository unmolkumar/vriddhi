Mareezon ka BP, pulse, temperature aur oxygen har ghante check karke chart mein likhti hoon.
Doctor ke order ke hisaab se injection aur IV drip lagati hoon aur dawai ka reaction dekhti rehti hoon.
Ghav ki dressing, catheter aur Ryles tube daalna, blood sample lena - yeh sab roz ka kaam hai.
Discharge ke time mareez aur ghar walon ko samjhati hoon ki ghar par dawai aur ghav ka dhyan kaise rakhna hai.
B.Sc Nursing, Delhi Nursing Council mein registered.
